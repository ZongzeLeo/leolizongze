Wearable bio-adhesive metal detector array (BioMDA) for spinal implants

Li, J., Jia, S., Li, D., Chow, L., Zhang, Q., Yang, Y., Bai, X., Qu, Q., Gao, Y., Li, Z., Li, Z., Shi, R., Zhang, B., Huang, Y., Pan, X., Hu, Y., Gao, Z., Zhou, J., Park, W., & Huang, X.
Nature Communications, 15(1) 2024 doi:10.1038/s41467-024-51987-2 ↗
A skin-conformal, adhesive wearable array that non-invasively localizes metallic spinal implants — enabling continuous post-surgical monitoring without imaging equipment. The bio-adhesive design conforms to body contours and maintains stable signal acquisition under motion.

Magnetic repulsion-based robot with diverse locomotion capabilities

Wei, Z., Li, Z., Xiang, Y., Liu, J., & Zhang, J.
IEEE Robotics and Automation Letters, 9(1), 398–405 2024 IEEE Xplore ↗
A magnetically actuated miniature robot capable of multi-mode locomotion and seamless gait transitions, controlled via a custom Helmholtz coil platform. Real-time voltage waveform synthesis enables flexible field generation for diverse motion patterns.

Using decellularized magnetic microrobots to deliver functional cells for cartilage regeneration

Huang, H., Li, J., Wang, C., Xing, L., Cao, H., Wang, C., Chung Yan Leung, Li, Z., Xi, Y., Tian, H., Li, F., & Sun, D.

RF-based quantitative ultrasound attenuation pipeline for MASLD staging with automated ROI selection

Li, Z., [co-authors TBD], Salcudean, T.
IEEE Transactions on Ultrasonics, Ferroelectrics, and Frequency Control Under review
A fully automated RF-based quantitative ultrasound pipeline for non-invasive assessment of metabolic dysfunction-associated steatotic liver disease (MASLD). The method demonstrates strong agreement with MRI-PDFF (n=35, Pearson r=0.924) and high diagnostic performance for steatosis grading (AUC ≥ 0.95).
